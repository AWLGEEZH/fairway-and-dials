# Fairway & Dials: product roadmap

The watch lineup is released as a round of golf. Each release is the next stage of the round, so the collection tells one story from the first tee to the 19th hole and gives every launch its own announcement. Dial concepts for every model are in `designs/dials/`.

## Release order

| # | Release | Collection | Stage of the round | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Tee Box** | Links | The opening drive | Launch model. The most distinctive dial, with tee hour markers, a ball on the 12 and the pin-in-the-cup easter egg every :30. |
| 2 | **Caddie** | Links | The companion who walks every hole | Permanent core model, kept in stock after launch. The everyday watch of the range. |
| 3 | **Fairway Stripe** | Links | The good shot | First "round" release after the core is set. |
| 4 | **Bunker** | Links | The first trouble | Sand dial; the most photogenic of the motifs. |
| 5 | **Marker** | Tool | Keeping score | First Tool collection watch: stroke-counter bezel and hole-counter inner ring. Needs its own case, so it follows proven Links demand. |
| 6 | **Front Nine** | Links | The turn | Midpoint of the series. |
| 7 | **Pin High** | Links | On the green | Flagstick at 12, cup at 6. |
| 8 | **Clubhouse** | Links | The 19th hole | The dressy finale, with applied brass indices and a hidden "19" as the payoff for collectors who followed the series. |
| — | **Dawn Patrol** | Links | The early tee time | A limited edition or off-season release that can drop between any two releases above. |

## Pacing

- One release every 4 to 6 months, one dial run at a time. The full series takes about two and a half to three and a half years.
- Each release ships no date first (NH38). A date version (NH35) follows only if buyers ask for it.
- Caddie stays in the lineup permanently. The other Links models can be limited runs.

## Before the first release

These are the gates for Tee Box, tracked as GitHub issues:

1. Waitlist demand check, with Tee Box as the hero image (#22).
2. Mod prototype to test the case, hands and dial at real size (#19).
3. Swing testing on the course (#20).
4. Factory sample quotes from two or three assemblers (#21).

## Later

- **Tool collection:** Marker is the first model. Graphite is the collection's house color. "Water Hazard," "Out of Bounds" and "Rough" are name candidates for future Tool watches.
- **Pro Shop:** the name for the store section of the site and for limited editions or custom-dial commissions, not a watch.
- **Looper:** a candidate name for a limited Caddie edition.
- **Names:** every model name is a working title until a USPTO search in Class 14 clears it.
